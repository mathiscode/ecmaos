The FIGlet fonts the figlet command can import (core/kernel/public/importable-fonts) are distributed by figlet.js
and come from the FIGlet font collections. Each font's header names its designer and the terms they gave (many say
the font is free to use and redistribute with the header intact, some ask for credit, some are public domain). They
have not been reviewed one by one: use a font under the terms in its own header, and keep the header.
